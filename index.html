How to run it

  • Lifting days. Three or four exercises, 3 sets of 10. Pick the last two reps to feel genuinely hard, otherwise the weight is too light.
  • Going up. When all 3 sets hit 10 clean reps, add 1 to 2 kg next time. That is the whole method.
  • Zone 2. Incline high enough that you are breathing but could still hold a conversation. Speed matters less than incline.
  • Sore legs on a walk day? Keep the walk, drop the incline. Moving beats skipping.
  • Missed a day? Do not double up. Pick the plan back up on the next day as written.
